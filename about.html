About CardForge

A simple browser-based tool to create digital business cards with QR codes — built for students and professionals.

Features
Everything you need in one place

CardForge gives you a complete toolkit to build, customize, and share your professional identity right from your browser. No downloads needed.

⬡

Live Preview

See your business card update in real time as you type in the fields.

◈

QR Code Generation

Generate a scannable QR code with your full deployed card URL for mobile viewing.

❑

8 Card Themes

Choose from 8 color themes — dark gold, midnight blue, forest green, and more.

⌨

Font Styles

Pick from Serif, Sans-serif, or Monospace to match your personal brand.

✉

PNG Download

Download your generated card as a PNG image directly from the saved cards list.

↑

Native Sharing

Use the device share sheet to send your card via WhatsApp, email, or any app.

How It Works
Four steps to your perfect card
1

Fill in your details

Enter your name, job title, company, contact info, and social links in the form panel.

2

Customize the style

Pick a color theme and font style. Watch your card update live in the preview.

3

Generate your QR Code

Click "Generate QR" to create a scannable code containing all your contact information.

4

Share it anywhere

Scan the QR code, open the deployed card page on mobile, or download the card as PNG.

Tech Stack
Built with the fundamentals

CardForge is built entirely with standard web technologies — no backend, no Firebase, and no database. It works as a static GitHub Pages project.

HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript localStorage Sample JSON Login GitHub Pages QRCode.js html2canvas
